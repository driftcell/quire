Mount an attachment uploaded to this session (`attachment_id`) into the run's sandbox at `/workspace/attachments/{attachment_id}/{filename}`. Returns the sandbox `path`, `filename`, `mime_type`, and `size_bytes` (storage credentials and signed URLs are never exposed). After mounting, read small text files (<=256 KiB) with `sandbox_read_file` or parse spreadsheets/PDFs/large files with `sandbox_execute`.
